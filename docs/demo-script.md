# demo script: fifteen seconds

for a screen recording to post at launch. use the example year, or your own files with names hidden.

1. **0 to 2 s.** the landing page, light mode. the headline "your year, from your own data." in view.
2. **2 to 4 s.** drag a spotify zip from the desktop onto the drop zone. the zone lights up.
3. **4 to 6 s.** "reading your files" with real counts climbing: "spotify: reading 41,000 plays".
4. **6 to 7 s.** "what we found": the spotify row, the year, "make my year".
5. **7 to 12 s.** the deck. press the right arrow every second: hours, top artists (names hidden), your hour on the clock, the biggest day on the calendar, on repeat.
6. **12 to 15 s.** the last card, "your year in one page", then "save image" and the saved image opening.

caption for the post: "my year, from my own data. it never left my laptop."

tips
- turn wi-fi off before recording and leave the network icon in view.
- record at 1280 by 800, then crop to 4:5 for the post.
- leave names hidden. the cards still read well with bullets for names.
